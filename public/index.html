Services

Four things we do well. Nothing we don’t.

Every engagement ends with code in your repo, an eval set that proves it works, and a team that can run it without us.

01 / AGENTS

Custom AI Agents

Agents that do real work inside your systems: read tickets, query databases, call your APIs and take actions — with guardrails, tracing and a human in the loop where it matters.

What you get
  • Typed tools with explicit permission boundaries
  • Golden set + CI regression runs; a pass-rate you can quote
  • Tracing, cost and latency dashboards from the first deploy
  • Review queues and escalation paths for low-confidence runs
02 / RETRIEVAL

Search, RAG & Reranking

Retrieval that returns the right thing, not just something. Hybrid search, chunking strategy, rerankers and evals tuned to your actual corpus and query logs.

What you get
  • Hybrid BM25 + vector retrieval on Postgres/pgvector or your existing store
  • Cross-encoder or LLM reranking stage, benchmarked against baseline
  • Eval harness: recall@k, nDCG, groundedness, answer accuracy
  • Ingestion pipeline with freshness, dedupe and access control
03 / PRODUCT

Custom Software & SaaS

Full products end to end: data model, API, web app, auth, billing, deploys. A boring, reliable stack chosen for the next five years, not the next conference.

What you get
  • Next.js / TypeScript front end, Python or Node backend
  • A Postgres schema you will still like in two years
  • CI/CD, staging, monitoring and backups configured, not promised
  • Admin tools and audit logs from day one
04 / INTEGRATION

AI Integration & Automation

Put models into the workflows you already run — CRM, helpdesk, ERP, spreadsheets, email — without ripping anything out or retraining your team.

What you get
  • Document extraction and classification pipelines with validation
  • Queue-based automations with retries, idempotency and alerts
  • Claude / OpenAI / open-weights routing with fallbacks
  • Cost caps, usage reporting and per-team budgets

FAQ

Questions we get on the first call.

Something not here? Email hello@reranklab.com — a person replies, within one business day.
Q.01How long does a project take?
A prototype takes 3–10 days. Most builds take 2–8 weeks depending on integrations and how much of the product already exists. You get a dated plan after the discovery call, and weekly demos against it.
Q.02How much does it cost?
Prototypes typically start around US$5k. Full builds usually land in the US$15k–50k range depending on scope; larger platforms go beyond that. Everything is quoted fixed-price up front, so the number you sign is the number you pay.
Q.03Who owns the IP?
You do. Code, prompts, eval data, infrastructure — all in your repositories and cloud accounts from the first commit, with no licensing back to us. If we part ways, you keep everything and it keeps running.
Q.04Which models do you use?
Whatever fits the job. Claude and OpenAI models cover most agent and extraction work; open-weights models (Llama, Qwen) when data residency or unit cost demands it. We benchmark on your data and build the model layer so swapping later is a config change, not a rewrite.
Q.05Do you maintain it after launch?
Optionally. Many clients keep us on a monthly retainer for monitoring, eval tuning and new features. Others take the handover docs and run it with their own team. Both work, and you can switch between them.
Q.06How do we start?
Fill in the form below or email hello@reranklab.com with a paragraph about the problem. You will get a reply within one business day and a 45-minute call the same week. If we are not the right fit, we will say so and point you somewhere better.
Q.07What does “fixed price” actually mean?
Before the build we agree a written scope and an acceptance number: an eval pass-rate on a golden set we build together, plus a latency and cost budget where they matter. When the number is met, the project is done and the invoice is the one you signed. Changes to scope are priced and agreed in writing, never discovered on an invoice.
Q.08What don’t you do?
Mobile apps, marketing websites, hourly staff augmentation, and “AI strategy” engagements with no code at the end. We also do not take retainers before something is shipped and measured. If your project is one of these, we will say so on the first call and suggest someone better suited.
